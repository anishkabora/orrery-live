How to explore

Orrery Live

DRAG
Orbit the camera around whatever is in focus.
PINCH · WHEEL
Zoom from the Solar System down to a single satellite.
TAP
Select and fly to an object. On Earth, tap the surface to inspect that spot.
2-FINGER · RIGHT-DRAG
Pan.
TIME
Speed time up or reverse it; NOW returns to the present.
SLICE OPEN
Focus a planet, Moon or the Sun, then press Slice open in the toolbar at the bottom. Choose Wedge or Half and tap a layer.
SCANNER
Zoom close to a world: the crosshair reads out what is under it, including local time, sunlight, cloud band, wind and the nearest named feature.

Planet, Moon and satellite positions are computed live in your browser. Distances and sizes are compressed so everything fits; switch to True scale to feel the emptiness.
